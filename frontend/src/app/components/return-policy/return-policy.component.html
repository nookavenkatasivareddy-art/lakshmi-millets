Return & Refund Policy

We are here to help if your order arrives damaged or incorrect.

Eligible issues

Because we sell packaged food products, returns are not accepted once an order has been delivered. However, we will help if an item arrives damaged, expired, or different from what you ordered.

How to request help

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Please share your order number and clear photographs of the package and product so our team can review the issue.

Replacement or refund

After verification, we may arrange a replacement or refund depending on product availability and the nature of the issue. Approved refunds are returned using the original payment method where possible.
