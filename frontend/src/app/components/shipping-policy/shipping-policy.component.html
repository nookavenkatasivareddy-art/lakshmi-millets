Shipping Policy

Simple, reliable delivery for your Lakshmi Millets order.

Delivery information

We process orders after confirmation and deliver them to the saved delivery address selected at checkout. Estimated delivery time and any applicable delivery charge are displayed before you place your order.

Delivery charges

Orders above ₹499 (items total) qualify for free delivery. For orders below ₹499, the delivery charge shown at checkout applies.

Order updates

Use the Track Order page or My Orders section after logging in to view your latest order status. Please ensure your delivery address and phone number are accurate when placing the order.
