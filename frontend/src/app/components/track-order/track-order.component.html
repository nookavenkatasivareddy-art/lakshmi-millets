Track Your Order

Stay updated from order confirmation to delivery.

How to track your order

Log in to your Lakshmi Millets account and open My Orders. Select the order you want to check to see its current status, items, delivery address, payment method, and total.

Order status meanings

  • Placed: We have received your order.
  • Confirmed: Your order is being prepared.
  • Shipped: Your package is on its way.
  • Delivered: Your order has reached the delivery address.
